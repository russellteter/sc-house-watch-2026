# SC House Watch 2026

register: product

## Product purpose
A single-page, election-night results tracker for the South Carolina State House (all 124 seats), answering one question live: can Democrats break the Republican two-thirds supermajority (83 seats; Democrats break it at 42)? Also tracks 14 Key Flip Targets, HD 115 (defend), the Charleston-area starred races, and the U.S. Senate race county by county. Used for one night (3 Nov 2026), then retired.

## Users
Russell Teter (owner) and the people he shares the link with: Democratic organizers, candidates' teams and politically engaged friends in South Carolina. They check it repeatedly over a few hours on laptops and phones, often while watching TV coverage. They want the current state in two seconds, then detail on demand.

## Tone
Newsroom results desk: plain, factual, unofficial-results caveats, no cheerleading in the chrome (the headline verdict may read good/bad for Democrats, but numbers lead). Plain English, every term glossed inline.

## Strategic principles
- Accuracy and freshness over everything: official county feeds, every 2 minutes, with freshness and reporting figures always visible.
- The verdict first, then the scorecard, then detail. Nothing above the fold should need scrolling to understand.
- Dense but calm: one visual idiom for status colours (Dem blue, Rep red, close amber, undecided grey), reused everywhere.
- Progressive disclosure: large visuals (seating chart, maps, county tables) start compact and expand on request.

## Anti-references
- Cable-news dashboards with flashing tickers and five competing charts.
- Campaign-site styling (hero banners, rallying copy).
- Generic SaaS dashboard cards with big-number tiles and gradients.
